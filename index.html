Questions

Frequently
Asked

How do you source access to sold-out events?
IALB Seats works with a curated network of verified hospitality providers and access partners built over time. Every allocation is confirmed in writing before you are asked to pay a single euro.
Why don't you show prices on the website?
Because no two experiences are the same. Prices reflect the specific event, seat location, inclusions, and timing of your request. We send you a tailored proposal within 24 hours of your enquiry — one that reflects exactly what you are looking for.
How quickly do you respond?
We respond to all enquiries within 2 hours, Monday to Saturday. For urgent, same-day requests, please contact us directly via WhatsApp and we will prioritise your enquiry immediately.
Do you serve international clients?
Yes — we regularly arrange experiences for clients across Europe, the Middle East, and beyond. We handle all logistics, ensure digital delivery of access, and provide full pre-event briefings regardless of your location.
Can you arrange group bookings?
Absolutely. We specialise in group and corporate hospitality, ensuring adjacent seating and a seamless, coordinated experience. Simply mention the group size and any preferences in your enquiry.
What happens if my event is cancelled?
If your event is cancelled and not rescheduled, you are entitled to a full refund. We manage the entire process — you simply notify us and we handle everything from that point forward.
